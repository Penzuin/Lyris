Wayfarer's Breath

Jumping, sprinting and climbing in Lyris all draw from one stamina pool, the Breath. Play with it below, then read the numbers and the build notes.

All values are starting points for playtesting. Distances use Unreal units at 100 per metre.

Recovery

  • Refill waits 0.8 s after the last spend.
  • Then ramps from 20/s to 50/s over 1.5 s. A full bar back from empty takes about 3 s.
  • No refill while climbing or sprinting. Refill in the air is allowed.

Running dry

  • At 0 you are Winded: sprint and climbing lock, refill runs at 70%.
  • Winded on a wall means you slide off. No fall damage under 6 m, so cliffs feel brave, not punishing.
  • Winded clears at 35%. The bar marks that point with a small gold notch.

Feel helpers

  • Coyote time 0.12 s and jump buffer 0.15 s.
  • Variable jump: letting go early halves upward speed.
  • Air control at 60% of ground acceleration.

The bar

Most games show a plain wedge that fills and empties. The Breath ring sits beside the character's head, hides when full, and tries to tell you five things at a glance instead of one.

Living colourWarm gold when full, deepening to amber and ember as you tire. Below 25% the ring breathes with a slow glow instead of a harsh flash.
Drain trailA pale ghost arc lingers where Breath just was, then catches up. You can read how much that last leap cost.
Breath vialThe centre is a small glass vial whose water level and ripples match the ring. It cracks when you are Winded.
Cost previewWhile climbing, the slice a leap would spend is outlined. If you cannot afford it, the outline turns red before you try.
Tea haloFood and drink add a thin outer ring of bonus Breath that is spent first, so the main bar stays honest.
Recovery notchWhen Winded, a notch at 35% shows exactly when sprint and climbing return.

Growth: Academy training adds max Breath in steps of 20 (100 → 200). Each step shows as an extra tick on the ring rather than a longer bar, so the ring never changes size on screen.

Building it in Unreal

Player lane

  • ULyrisStaminaComponent on the character: Current, Max, Bonus, LastSpendTime, bWinded. Exposes TrySpend(), CanAfford() and an OnStaminaChanged delegate.
  • Sprint and jump stay in UCharacterMovementComponent; climbing is a MOVE_Custom mode with wall-normal tracing and a ledge trace for the mantle.
  • Tuning lives in a ULyrisMovementTuning data asset so designers change numbers without code.
  • Server-authoritative stamina for multiplayer, with client prediction for sprint drain.

UI lane

  • One UMG widget with a single material: radial arc, ghost arc, preview arc and vial driven by scalar parameters.
  • Widget is screen-projected beside the head socket and binds to OnStaminaChanged; no ticking logic in the widget.
  • Colour ramp in a curve asset so the Storybook palette can be tuned by eye.
